Q: What maintenance is required?

### Industry Terminology Definitions:

- Battery Cycle: Complete charge/discharge sequence, affects battery lifespan
- DOT Compliance: Department of Transportation requirements for street legal vehicles
- Payload Capacity: Maximum weight the vehicle can safely carry
- Regenerative Braking: System that recovers energy during braking to extend range
- Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4): Advanced battery technology with longer life
- Controller: Electronic brain that manages motor power and performance
- Differential: Mechanism allowing wheels to turn at different speeds during turns
- Ground Clearance: Distance between lowest point and ground, important for terrain
