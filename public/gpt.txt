### Golf Cart Classification Guide

LSV (Low Speed Vehicle):
- Maximum speed: 25 mph
- Street legal on roads with 35 mph limit or less
- Requires DOT-approved safety equipment
- Must be registered and insured in Rhode Island

NEV (Neighborhood Electric Vehicle):
- Similar to LSV with street-legal capabilities
- Ideal for community use and short trips

Golf Cart (Non-Street Legal):
- For private property, golf courses, gated communities
- No street-legal requirements
- Generally lower cost

PTV (Personal Transportation Vehicle):
- Versatile for various applications
- Can be converted to street-legal

### Rhode Island Golf Cart Laws Summary

- Golf carts may operate on roads with speed limits of 35 mph or less
- Must have all required safety equipment for street use
- Operators must have a valid driver's license
- Golf carts must be registered with VA DMV
- Liability insurance required for street use
- Local ordinances may have additional requirements
