### Problem-Solution Training Pairs:

Problem: "My golf cart won't charge"
Solution: Check power outlet, inspect charger connection, clean battery terminals, verify charger is working. If issue persists, bring cart to our service center.

Problem: "Range has decreased significantly"
Solution: Check tire pressure (low pressure reduces range), battery age (3-5 year lifespan), weather conditions (cold reduces range), ensure full charge cycles.

Problem: "Cart is slower than usual"
Solution: Check battery charge level, tire pressure, brake adjustment. May need service if persistent.

Problem: "Need street legal conversion"
Solution: We install all required equipment: headlights, taillights, brake lights, turn signals, mirrors, seat belts, horn. Handle DMV paperwork and registration.
